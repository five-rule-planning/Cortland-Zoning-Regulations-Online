Accessory Uses and Supplemental Regulations

Accessory Building

Buildings and structures may be erected and land may be used for purposes which are clearly incidental to, and customarily and commonly associated with the main permitted use of the premises. Such accessory buildings and uses shall be so constructed, maintained and conducted as to not produce noise, vibration, concussion, dust, dirt, fly ash, odor, noxious gases, heat or glare which is injurious, damaging, unhealthful or disturbing to adjacent property, or the users thereof, and shall be on the premises of the main use.

The following conditions and restrictions shall apply to accessory buildings in all residential zones:

  1. One detached accessory building and one minor tool or storage building may be located on the premises.
  2. A detached accessory building shall not exceed one hundred twenty (120) square feet of floor area for every one thousand (1,000) feet of lot area.
  3. A minor storage building shall not exceed 300 square feet in area
  4. The required minimum setbacks for detached buildings are shown in the following table
Adjacent Feature Front Set-Back Side Set-Back Rear Set-Back
Lot N/A 5 Feet 5 Feet
Street R-O-W 25 Feet 15 Feet 15 Feet
Alley R-O-W N/A 3 Feet 3 Feet
Main Dwelling N/A 8 Feet 8 Feet

Accessory buildings shall not be located any closer to the street than the building line of the main Structure and no closer than 8 feet to the main structure.

  1. All buildings having one hundred and twenty (120) square feet or more of floor area shall be of exterior materials and general design so as to be equal and or harmonious with the appearance of the main dwelling.
  2. All buildings constructed or relocated will require a building permit.
  3. All garage entrances must have a minimum ten (10) feet long drive when garage opening is perpendicular to the access street or alley. Attached garages are considered part of the principal building.
  4. Accessory buildings shall be on same tax parcel as the primary structure, on the same or adjoining lots, and not separated by streets or alleyways.

Shipping and Storage Containers

Cargo shipping containers shall not be allowed inside of the City Limits on a permanent basis as an accessory building. On a case by case basis, a permanent special use permit can be applied for. If a special use permit is issued, the shipping container must be placed on a cement pad, and painted to cover any advertising, and the container must be locked securely at all times.

Shipping containers already in place at the time of passing of this ordinance may remain in place, but if removed, they cannot be returned. Shipping containers in place however, should be painted if they have signage.

Temporary shipping containers may be used for short term storage such as: construction or remodeling sites. The zoning administrator should be notified and an estimate time frame of need should be reported. Temporary shipping containers shall be placed to not create any traffic hazards.
